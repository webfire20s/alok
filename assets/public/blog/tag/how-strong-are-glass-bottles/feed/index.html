Strength and Durability of Glass Bottles



The strength of glass bottles is due to the strength of the chemical bonds between the atoms in the glass. These bonds are incredibly strong and can withstand a lot of force. Glass bottles are also typically made with thick walls that help to distribute the pressure evenly across the surface of the bottle.
